I build research systems that search for trading strategies on their own, and I run the firms that put them to work.

**Founder, Futuristic Group.** Futuristic is a research lab that trades its own book, with tokenized real-world assets as its main line. Its work is a self-evolving research engine: hypotheses are generated and tested automatically, while the evaluation protocol stays in human hands and the engine is not permitted to edit it. Unseen data is treated as a consumable budget — every read is logged against a quota, and a window that has been opened is never returned to the search. Strategies are the output; the engine is the asset.

**Researcher, AI for research — with quantitative finance as the testbed.** Automated search makes discovery cheap and makes judgement the binding constraint. Finance is an unusually honest place to study that: signals decay, unseen data is genuinely scarce, and being wrong is priced daily. What I am working on is on the [research](/research/) page.

**Co-founder, Quanta Edge.** Ultra-low-latency trading in Chinese index futures and equity markets with FPGA-based execution, and high-frequency market making for tokenized real-world assets.

I am also a PhD candidate at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology. Before that I worked on AI research at Megvii and on reinforcement-learning trading models at Graphen in New York. I hold degrees in Engineering Physics (Tsinghua University), Economics (Peking University) and Computer Science (M.S., Columbia University). A fuller record is on my [CV](/cv/).

Most of my current work lives in private repositories.
