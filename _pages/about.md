<section class="beliefs" markdown="1">

## What I believe
{: .label}

<div class="claims" markdown="1">

<div class="claim" markdown="1">
01
{: .claim-n}
**A strategy is a depreciating asset.**
{: .claim-h}
Agents will soon propose more candidates in a day than a market can absorb in a year, and the half-life of a signal will fall from years to weeks. A firm that owns strategies owns inventory. The only thing worth owning is the process that regenerates them, and the record of what each one was tested against.
</div>

<div class="claim" markdown="1">
02
{: .claim-n}
**Hardware goes the same way.**
{: .claim-h}
Today the low-latency stack is a fixed platform and strategies adapt to it. Soon the design is rebuilt with the strategy: one strategy, one piece of silicon, searched by agents and judged on the device. The engineer's job becomes writing the constraints and the tests, not the design.
</div>

<div class="claim" markdown="1">
03
{: .claim-n}
**What cannot be generated decides the winner.**
{: .claim-h}
Unseen market data. A build that closes timing. A venue to act on. None of these can be manufactured; they are spent. The firm that rations them best, and spends them only on candidates that have earned it, wins.
</div>

</div>

<section class="building" markdown="1">

## What I am building
{: .label}

**Quanta Edge** is that firm. A handful of people write the exam; agents write everything else, the strategies and the hardware alike. The evaluation protocol is frozen and out of the agents' reach. Every read of unseen data is logged against a budget, every chip is judged by the latency it actually delivers, and every position that goes live carries a receipt back to the test that authorised it.
{: .building-text}

<section class="questions" markdown="1">

## Questions we are stuck on
{: .label}

<div class="qs" markdown="1">

<div class="q" markdown="1">
**Can a check carry information?**
{: .q-h}
Most of the time a candidate's evidence is the same size as the noise the search itself produces. We can measure how much performance a pipeline manufactures out of nothing. We cannot yet build a check we trust at that scale.
</div>

<div class="q" markdown="1">
**Where is the line, and how do you know it was crossed?**
{: .q-h}
The search may rewrite itself. The judge and the data layer must not move. We know where we drew the line. We do not know how to tell, from inside the system, that it has been crossed.
</div>

<div class="q" markdown="1">
**How much of the judge can be learned?**
{: .q-h}
A full build takes hours; an unseen window is spent once. Surrogates are cheap, and the agent will learn to fool them. What has to be measured, and what may be predicted?
</div>
