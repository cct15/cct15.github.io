<header class="page-header">
  <p class="eyebrow">Research</p>
  <h1>Deep learning and AI agents in finance</h1>
  <p class="lead">I’m interested in what models can learn from market data and how agents can use the outcome of one experiment to design a better one. Financial markets give me a setting where predictions can be recorded in advance and checked against new data.</p>
</header>

<section class="section" aria-labelledby="directions-title">
  <div class="section-heading"><p class="eyebrow">Research questions</p><h2 id="directions-title">Search, evaluation and correction</h2></div>
  <div class="research-directions">
    <article><h3>Parallel discovery</h3><p>I study how agents can share tools and useful findings while pursuing independent lines of inquiry. I want to know when sharing helps a group find ideas that a single search would miss.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Independent evaluation</h3><p>I want an agent to state what it expects before seeing the outcome. The goal is to test those expectations on evidence kept separate from discovery and make the evaluation reproducible.</p></article>
    <article><h3>Learning from failure</h3><p>I study whether an agent can tell a flawed experiment from a flawed hypothesis, then change its research method. A further test is whether the revised method helps on a different task.</p></article>
  </div>
</section>

<section class="section" aria-labelledby="evaluation-title">
  <div class="research-principle">
    <h2 id="evaluation-title">When should an agent trust its own price prediction?</h2>
    <p>I want agents to record what they predict, over what horizon and with what confidence. Comparing those records with later outcomes lets me ask whether an agent’s confidence is justified and whether it stays reliable when market conditions change.</p>
  </div>
</section>
