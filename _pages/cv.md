Experience
======
* **Futuristic Group Ltd** — Founder, 2025–present. Hong Kong.
  * Founded a research lab that trades its own book, running systematic strategies and on-chain market making, with tokenized real-world assets as its main line.
  * The lab's work is a self-evolving research engine: hypotheses are generated and tested automatically under a frozen evaluation protocol the engine cannot modify, with unseen data managed as a logged, depletable budget.
  * Built the research and trading stack end to end: data infrastructure across venues, a validation framework with walk-forward testing and a pre-deployment audit, and live monitoring that ties realised PnL back to the backtest that authorised the position.
  * Lead strategy research, and set the standard a signal has to clear before it is allowed to trade capital.

* **Quanta Edge** — Co-founder, 2026–present.
  * Ultra-low-latency trading in Chinese index futures and equity markets, with FPGA-based execution on colocated infrastructure.
  * High-frequency market making for tokenized real-world assets on-chain, together with the low-latency execution stack beneath it.
  * Set the research agenda on the strategy side and build out the trading and engineering team.

* **Graphen, Inc.**, Market Intelligence — Research Assistant, 2020. New York, USA.
  * Reinforcement-learning models for high-frequency FX trading, working on model stability and return.
  * Introduced Reinforcement Sensitivity Theory from psychology into the trading model, matching client risk profiles to strategies.

* **Megvii**, Algorithm Department — Algorithm Intern, 2018–2019. Beijing, China.
  * Fundamental research on neural networks, focused on the robustness of deep networks used for face recognition.
  * Integrated a decision function into the existing model for optimisation and sample visualisation.
